## Source Material — `sd-fxmap-math-tutor` skill

This tutor skill is based on the publicly shared educational work of **Andrei Zelenco** — landscape architect turned 3D artist, whose distinctive contribution to the Substance Designer community is a mathematical and systems-oriented approach to procedural materials (FX-Map, Pixel Processor, math expressions, vector field equations, sine / Fourier patterns, reaction-diffusion).

### Creator Information

- **Creator**: Andrei Zelenco
- **Background**: Landscape architect → 3D artist; brings parametric-design thinking (Grasshopper / Inventor lineage) to Substance Designer
- **Pedagogical signature**: Treats Substance Designer as a vehicle for visual programming — emphasis on *why* the math works before *how* the nodes connect

## Purpose

This plugin teaches Claude how to tutor Substance Designer concepts. It curates educational knowledge from publicly available creator content to help 3D artists learn procedural texture creation through conversation, then directs them back to the original creators for deeper learning.
