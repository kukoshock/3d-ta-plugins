## What this article actually is

This is an **announcement / news article** by 80.lv promoting Andrei Zelenco's free four-part FX-Map tutorial series. It is *not* itself a technical guide. The article gives a short framing of FX-Map ("one of the most powerful Substance 3D nodes" and "one of the most complex"), summarizes what the four-video series progresses through (basics → math expressions → nested loops → branching → vector expressions → iterated functions → fully procedural drawings), and links to Andrei's ArtStation. The substantive technical content lives in Andrei's videos, not in this article.
